Let’s bring digital literacy closer to home! *🎯 Task: Build a User-Friendly Chatbot for Our Digital Literacy Program* *Guidelines:* 👉 Design a chatbot specifically for the *Digital Literacy Program Website* with a focus on helping parents and older users learn and grow in the digital era. 👉 Ensure the chatbot is intuitive, easy to operate, and accessible for users who may not be tech-savvy. --- *Steps to Design Your Chatbot* *1. Chatbot Personality:* - Create a friendly and welcoming bot persona, like *"DigiBuddy"*, that talks in simple, relatable language. - Add quirky lines to make it approachable, e.g., *“Hello! I’m DigiBuddy, your tech-savvy buddy. Let’s master the digital world together!”* *2. Basic Functionalities:* - *FAQ Section:* - Answer common questions like: - *“What is a browser?”* - *“How can I set up an email account?”* - *Interactive Tutorials:* - Provide step-by-step guidance for basic tasks: - How to pay bills online. - How to use WhatsApp and other social media apps. - *Troubleshooting Help:* - Offer solutions for common digital issues like: - *“Why isn’t my phone connecting to Wi-Fi?”* *3. Accessibility Features:* - Use larger fonts and simple icons for easier visibility. - Include voice assistance for users who prefer speaking to typing. - Build options for multi-language support to cater to diverse users. *4. Content Focus:* - Simplify jargon and keep answers short. - Use examples and analogies for explanations, e.g., *“An email is like sending a letter, but digitally!”* - Add sections like *“Quick Tech Tips for Parents”* or *“Digital World Basics.”* *5. Easy Navigation:* - Make the chatbot interface straightforward with buttons like: - *“Learn Basics”* - *“Ask a Question”* - *“Get Help”* --- *Tools Recommended for the Task* - *Dialogflow (Google)*: To design conversational flows with natural language processing. - *ChatGPT API (OpenAI)*: For creating dynamic and personalized responses. - *Botpress*: To build user-friendly chatbot interfaces. - *Voiceflow*: To add voice-based interaction. *Deliverables* 1.
